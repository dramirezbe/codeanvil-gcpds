- **STK / NRM:** priority taken directly from the block's `Priority` field.

## CG-01 — RF Acquisition & Front-End Integrity

### `STK-EMC-001` — Sensor EMC optimization

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.1.1 Electromagnetic Compatibility
- **Statement:** The system SHALL incorporate filtering in the sensor's receive path and RF shielding to prevent spurious signals generated by its own electronics from appearing in measurement results.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Internal spurious emissions are ≥ 10 dB below the operating noise floor. (2) RF shielding is implemented between internal modules. (3) A before/after comparative measurement demonstrates reduction in EMC artifacts.
- **Notes:** The HackRF lacks pre-selection filters, so its broadband front-end is susceptible to internal EMI. An RF gasket + board-level shielding provides physical mitigation. The DSP pipeline characterizes known internal spurious frequencies and flags them.

### `STK-RNG-001` — Frequency-range coverage

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.1.2 Spectral Range
- **Statement:** The system SHALL capture emissions from 1 MHz up to 6 GHz.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) The system receives and processes signals in the 1 MHz – 6 GHz range. (2) Sensitivity at 1 MHz and 6 GHz is verifiable with a calibrated signal generator.
- **Notes:** Sensitivity degrades at band edges (below 10 MHz and above 4 GHz). Characterization per band is required.

### `STK-RNG-002` — Real-time monitoring bandwidth

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.1.2 Spectral Range
- **Statement:** The system SHALL provide real-time monitoring with a configurable bandwidth between 6 MHz and 20 MHz, and an automatic mode fixed at 20 MHz.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Bandwidth configurable between 6 MHz and 20 MHz in discrete steps. (2) In automatic mode, the span is fixed at 20 MHz. (3) DSP pipeline processes the full bandwidth with latency ≤ 1 second.
- **Notes:** At 20 MS/s, sample drops are possible due to the USB 2.0 bottleneck (~32 MB/s sustained vs. 40 MB/s IQ rate). The DSP pipeline tolerates or detects them.

- **Notes:** USB 2.0 theoretical: 480 Mbps = 60 MB/s. Practical sustained: ~32 MB/s. HackRF at 20 MS/s × 2 bytes = 40 MB/s exceeds this. Sample drops at sustained 20 MS/s are possible.

### `HWC-DRV-001` — IQ throughput budget (end-to-end)

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** CONSTRAINT · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.18 Derived Constraints

- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Notes:** The USB 2.0 bottleneck (~32 MB/s) is the weakest link in the 40 MB/s chain. Mitigations: (1) accept occasional drops in non-forensic mode, (2) reduce sample rate for continuous recording, (3) use burst capture for forensic storage.

## CG-10 — Calibration, Accuracy & Normative Compliance

### `STK-CAL-001` — Calibration procedures

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.1 Synchronization and Calibration
- **Statement:** The system SHALL include calibration procedures for frequency, receive gain, IQ balance, and DC-offset correction.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Documented frequency-calibration procedure. (2) Documented gain-calibration procedure. (3) Documented IQ-balance and DC-offset correction procedure. (4) Each calibration produces a dated record with results.
- **Notes:** Calibration uses external reference sources (signal generator) to build correction tables that compensate for gain uncertainty.

### `STK-CAR-001` — DANL

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.2 System Characterization
- **Statement:** The system SHALL achieve a DANL ≤ –100 dBm across the operating range.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Measured DANL ≤ –100 dBm over ≥ 90% of the frequency range. (2) Measurement performed with 50 Ω load and documented per band.
- **Notes:** Software averaging improves effective DANL at the cost of temporal resolution.

### `STK-CAR-002` — Dynamic range

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.2 System Characterization
- **Statement:** The system SHALL achieve a dynamic range ≥ 80 dB.
- **Traces / dependencies:** `STK-CAR-001`
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Measured dynamic range ≥ 80 dB in a two-tone test. (2) Test setup and results documented.
- **Notes:** The 8-bit ADC provides ~48 dB single-capture SFDR. The ≥ 80 dB target is achieved via gain-stepped captures at multiple LNA/VGA settings + spectral averaging.

### `STK-CAR-003` — Frequency accuracy

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.2 System Characterization
- **Statement:** The system SHALL achieve a frequency accuracy of ± 2 ppm.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Measured frequency error ≤ ± 2 ppm against a calibrated reference. (2) Measurement at room temperature (20–25 °C).
- **Notes:** The stock oscillator is ± 20 ppm. A TCXO replacement (± 0.5–2 ppm) or GPS-disciplined correction is used.

### `STK-CAR-004` — Oscillator aging

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.2 System Characterization
- **Statement:** The system SHALL meet a frequency drift due to oscillator aging ≤ 1 ppm/year.
- **Traces / dependencies:** `STK-CAR-003`
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Selected oscillator has aging spec ≤ 1 ppm/year. (2) Oscillator type and aging spec documented.

### `STK-CAR-005` — Amplitude accuracy

- **Priority:** MUST · **Type:** NON_FUNCTIONAL · **Source:** G0_StRS.md 3.10.2 System Characterization
- **Statement:** The system SHALL achieve a typical amplitude accuracy of ± 1 dB.
- **Traces / dependencies:** —
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Amplitude error ≤ ± 1 dB over ≥ 90% of the frequency range. (2) Amplitude linearity verified in 10 dB steps.
- **Notes:** Requires per-frequency, per-gain-setting calibration tables built using external reference sources.
